## Result 1 — `movhysti` is NOT the lever. Prediction WRONG.

| | movhysti=10 | movhysti=4 |
|---|---|---|
| escape rate (still finger) | 8.9% | **10.7%** |
| distribution | clusters at 2 and 5 | same clusters, longer tail |

No improvement, marginally worse. Part of that "worse" is sample size (2,608
reports vs 797 finds rarer tail events), but there is no improvement to argue

neutral change makes the next result unattributable.

RULED OUT: `movhysti` for the stationary case. Do not retry it alone.

## Result 2 — S2 is real but FAR milder than the single sample suggested

28 lift events, isolated properly this time (a lift = large delta followed by a
gap in the stream; the first attempt wrongly flagged mid-drag peaks).

Liftoff peak |raw| per lift:
`[3, 4, 7, 8, 6, 10, 5, 8, 5, 28, 18, 13, 10, 6, 9, 17, 16, 7, 37, 7, 12, 15, 13, 38, 10, 5, 16, 9]`

Median |raw| during normal dragging: **5**

- 18 of 28 lifts peak at 3-12 — indistinguishable from a normal drag frame, harmless.
- 10 of 28 peak at 13-38, i.e. 3-8x a normal frame. That is the symptom.
- 3 of 28 exceed 20.

**The 44 -> 72 -> 84 three-frame ramp from session 01 did NOT reproduce.** Worst
here is 38. Two candidate reasons, and they are confounded so neither is proven:
`movhystn=4` may have reduced it, OR that first sample was an outlier. Do not
claim credit for this until it is tested at `movhystn=16` again.

**The SHAPE varies, which matters for the fix.** Sometimes the peak is the final
frame (runs 10, 11, 24 — a clean ramp into the last report); sometimes it is
second-to-last with smaller frames after it (run 19: 16, **37**, 21, 15). So a
fixed 1-frame holdback helps in the first case and does nothing in the second.

CONCLUSION for the S2 fix design: a 1-frame `TP_LIFT_TOG` is a PARTIAL fix at
best, and the sensor-side route (T42 suppression / CONFTHR debounce) is the
better bet — PLAN.md already preferred it for adding no latency, and this data
supports that preference on effectiveness too.

## Regressions
None from `movhystn=4`, which stays. `movhysti=4` reverted, so no residual.

## Verdict
KEEP `movhystn=4`. REVERT `movhysti`. S2 fix design needs rework before it is
worth a flash.

## Ruled out
- `movhysti` alone, for the stationary case (measured, no improvement).
- A 1-frame liftoff holdback as a COMPLETE S2 fix (measured: peak is not always
  the final frame).
